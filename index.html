Workshop

Rank I unlocks the weapon so you can build it. Rank II and III unlock its in-battle upgrades. Archers come with rank I.

How to play

  • Pick a tower at the bottom of the screen and place it on free ground beside the path.
  • Tap a built tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Shieldmen and jarls wear armour that cuts the damage of every shot. Archers are weak against them; use the catapult and Thor's totem.
  • Ravens fly. The catapult and the fire altar cannot reach them.
  • Shamans heal nearby allies and can briefly disable a tower. Kill them first.
  • The frost rune barely hurts but slows. Rune of Return does not kill: it throws enemies back down the path, costing a whole wave half its progress.
  • Ballista spears everyone in its firing line: place it looking along a long straight stretch.
  • You start with archers only. Every other weapon has to be bought in the Workshop for silver: rank I lets you build it, rank II and III unlock its in-battle upgrades. Silver comes from cleared fjords, so replay an easy one if you are short.
  • Shieldmen lock into a shield wall: standing three or more together they gain extra armour, and each one splits into two raiders when it dies. Break the group up with splash damage.
  • Berserkers charge. Every few seconds they burst forward at more than double speed — a slow tower alone will not stop them.
  • Shamans sabotage. Besides healing, they knock out the nearest tower for a couple of seconds. A dark tower with green sparks is disabled.
  • Trees and boulders occupy a cell: you cannot build on them, but they show where the path is tight.
  • The “Call wave early” button pays gold for every second you save.
